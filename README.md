# SUKARYA — Personal & Professional Awwwards-Tier Website

A high-end, cinematic personal and professional portfolio for **Sukarya (Sukarya Sutan Marajo / Rangga Badar)** — veteran Indonesian Film & Television Director, Senior Actor, Screenwriter, and Executive Board Member of PB PARFI & KFT Indonesia with over 40 years of creative legacy.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Architecture

- **Aesthetic Direction**: High-fashion cinematic dark mode (`#07080A`, deep obsidian, warm gold/bronze `#E5A93C`, subtle film grain texture, anamorphic lens flares, and director's clapperboard aesthetics).
- **Interactive Director's Viewfinder Monitor**: Interactive SmallHD/ARRI EVF simulation allowing visitors to toggle between 6 camera feeds, switch aspect ratios (2.39:1 Cinemascope, 16:9 Broadcast, 4:3 Vintage), apply LUTs (Rec.709, Golden Hour, Film Noir B&W, Vintage TV), toggle rule-of-thirds grid & safe areas, and take snapshots with custom shutter sound & celebratory confetti.
- **Buttery Smooth Scroll**: Powered by **Lenis** (by Studio Freight) for 60fps momentum scrolling.
- **Custom Viewfinder Cursor**: Fluid trailing cursor mimicking an anamorphic viewfinder target, expanding dynamically over clickable triggers.
- **Web Audio Cinema Synthesizer**: Zero-dependency procedural Web Audio API foley engine for authentic wooden clapperboard snaps and shutter clicks (with mute toggle).
- **Official Credentials & Hologram 3D Tilt**: High-resolution showcases of his official **KFT Indonesia** (Lisensi Profesi Sutradara No. `3840/C/20/XIV/2024`) and **PB PARFI** Executive Board membership card (`AB .01.00051.90.20`).
- **Complete Master Filmography Catalogue**:
  - Filterable by role (*Sutradara: 44 titles*, *Aktor: 15+ titles*, *Semua*).
  - Searchable by title, broadcaster, production house, and year.
  - Quick broadcaster filters: TransTV, Indosiar, RCTI, Trans 7, MNC TV, TVRI.
  - Sortable by year (Newest to Classic 1980s / 1995).
- **Featured Poster Gallery & Lightbox**: Restored original poster artworks (*Saras 008*, *Police 86*, *Kisah 9 Wali*, *The Transmart*, *Cahaya Hikmah*, *Angry Boss*, *Pondok Pak Cus*, *Di Culik Malaikat*, *Hafalan Quran Maryam*, *Di Bawah Lindungan Hati*, *Ketika Iblis Mengaku Tuhan*).
- **Theater Legacy & Academic Pedagogy**: Journey starting from Teater Matahari (1982), Teater BP7, staging *Karto Loewak* (Volpone), *Juragan Darkum*, *Whattapa (?)* at Taman Ismail Marzuki (TIM), and teaching at IKIP Jakarta & STIE Gunadarma.
- **Direct Collaboration Terminal**: Pre-configured WhatsApp direct booking generator, quick copy for phone & email, and direct download for the official PDF portfolio.
